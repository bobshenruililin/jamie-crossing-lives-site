ALivelihoods and choices

Shenzhen draws some activities. Hong Kong routines remain.

Reasons to choose Shenzhen: parts and expertise; choice, space and price; preferred work and lower living costs; meals. Reasons some activities stay local: most shopping; crowding; a child’s activities leave little time.

Spent on the Mainland, the basket leans to services. In Hong Kong, to retail and dining.

Selected AlipayHK cross-border spenders, January–May 2024: services are 49% of recorded Mainland spending and 12% in Hong Kong; retail and dining together are 42% and 86%. Life services are approximately 80% of Mainland services spending. Mainland China is not Shenzhen alone.

Government intention · then a documented programme

Exploring GBA healthcare purchasing to ease pressure and waits.

The Hong Kong Government has said it will explore purchasing suitable healthcare services in the Greater Bay Area, taking account of feasibility and patients’ needs. This is a policy intention, not evidence that pressure or waiting times have fallen.

Government reply, 2 April 2025, paragraph (5) ↗

One existing pathway: the follow-up consultation pilot

Eligible Hospital Authority follow-up patients can receive care at HKU–Shenzhen Hospital, authorise access to records through eHealth, and return to HA follow-up on request according to clinical need. More than 5,900 had joined by January 2026.

Cross-border needs can create business—delivery, tuition, paperwork help—alongside public and community support. Which needs can businesses meet, and where is other support needed?

CRelationships and support

A home can move. Not every connection moves with it.

The resident works and rents in Hong Kong, with property, close family, friends and activities in Shenzhen. The guide moved home to Shenzhen and continues family visits in Hong Kong.

For the guide, a qualification was not the whole story.

Friends shared a qualification lead. She qualified, then reported reluctance to offer her work. Someone at the organisation helped.

Residence alone doesn’t show where people connect and participate.

  1. Existing ties keep visits and activities connected to one city.
  2. New contacts help people enter opportunities in another.

Hypothesis

Can lasting ties sustain participation after a move or a change in routine?

Residence, relationships and a sense of home can change separately—so follow spending, referrals and community participation apart from where someone lives.

Singapore–Johor

Which purchases move, and which ties remain?

Two countries, with different rules for work, residence and services. The questions travel; the answers need local evidence.

The RTS Link is a rail project separate from the Johor–Singapore Special Economic Zone. JS-SEZ ↗

Questions for Singapore–Johor: what people would buy or do in Johor Bahru, and why; whether spending, relationships and participation continue after a move; what new needs Johor providers would meet; and how changing demand, jobs, housing and services affect Johor residents. Forecast extra annual spending with the RTS Link: Singapore consumers in Johor Bahru S$1.05 billion; Johor Bahru consumers in Singapore S$756 million.

A very preliminary possibility: a Hong Kong–Shenzhen learning exchange for Singapore retailers and service businesses, followed by small local tests. Failed adaptations would matter as much as promising ones. This is an exploration, not a commitment.

What this project draws on

Sixteen individual and group conversation occasions, three Shenzhen trips, a Hong Kong–Shenzhen Innovation and Technology Park visit and a Sheung Shui walk with brief local chats (31 October 2026), read alongside studies, statistics and programme documents. It is an exploratory, Hong Kong-centred study, not a representative survey. The accounts illustrate overlapping ways of living; they are not population categories.

Official series, programme records, forecasts, surveys and proposals have different evidentiary roles, marked throughout by the evidence key. Causal claims would need suitable comparisons.

This edition (7 November 2026) incorporates Jamie’s website feedback and the finalized Script flow v4. It is a working draft for review, not a final approval by Jamie.

About the visuals

The miniature is authored illustration, not measured geography: places are arranged to explain, and no route, distance or journey frequency is measured. Relationship symbols identify reported kinds of ties, not possessions, their number or strength.

Field photographs were supplied by Bob and Jamie. The Singapore–Johor visit and the Shenzhen fieldwork are kept distinct. Captions describe visible context; no pictured person is identified as a research participant. The Futian help point’s operator was not confirmed and described referrals were not tested.

Locator maps made with Natural Earth (public domain). Fonts: Barlow, Barlow Condensed and Instrument Serif, under the SIL Open Font Licence.
