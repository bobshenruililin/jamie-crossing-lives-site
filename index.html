Hypothesis

Can lasting ties sustain participation after a move or a change in routine?

Residence, relationships and a sense of home can change separately—so follow spending, referrals and community participation apart from where someone lives.

Singapore–Johor

Which purchases move, and which ties remain?

Two countries, with different rules for work, residence and services. The questions travel; the answers need local evidence.

The RTS Link is a rail project separate from the Johor–Singapore Special Economic Zone. JS-SEZ ↗

Questions for Singapore–Johor: what people would buy or do in Johor Bahru, and why; whether spending, relationships and participation continue after a move; what new needs Johor providers would meet; and how changing demand, jobs, housing and services affect Johor residents. Forecast extra annual spending with the RTS Link: Singapore consumers in Johor Bahru S$1.05 billion; Johor Bahru consumers in Singapore S$756 million.

Jamie proposes a Hong Kong–Shenzhen learning exchange for Singapore retailers and service businesses, then small local tests—studying failed adaptations as closely as promising ones.

What this project draws on

At least fourteen individual and group conversations, three Shenzhen trips, a Hong Kong–Shenzhen Innovation and Technology Park visit and a Sheung Shui walk with brief local chats (31 October 2026), read alongside studies, statistics and programme documents. It is an exploratory, Hong Kong-centred study, not a representative survey. The accounts illustrate overlapping ways of living; they are not population categories.

Official series, programme records, forecasts, surveys and proposals have different evidentiary roles, marked throughout by the evidence key. Causal claims would need suitable comparisons.

This edition (5 November 2026) follows the presentation’s Script v3, checked 2 November 2026. It is a working draft for review, not a final approval by Jamie.

About the visuals

The miniature is authored illustration, not measured geography: places are arranged to explain, and no route, distance or journey frequency is measured. Relationship symbols identify reported kinds of ties, not possessions, their number or strength. The optional route game uses invented demand, capacities and rules.

Field photographs were supplied by Bob and Jamie. The Singapore–Johor visit and the Shenzhen fieldwork are kept distinct. Captions describe visible context; no pictured person is identified as a research participant. The Futian help point’s operator was not confirmed and described referrals were not tested.
