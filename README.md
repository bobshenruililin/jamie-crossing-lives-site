# When lives span a border

Working presentation and research companion for Jamie Ko's AsiaGlobal Fellows research.
Research and presentation: Jamie Ko. Visual support: Bob.

This public export contains audience-facing materials and public source links.
The full private research and authoring repository is separate.

The study is exploratory and Hong Kong-centred, informing questions for Singapore–Johor.
Statistics, programme descriptions, forecasts and proposals are distinguished throughout.
Comparative examples and future reading avenues do not establish transferable effects.

Open `index.html` through an HTTP server (`python3 -m http.server`) for the visual
research story; `/feature/` is an equivalent entry point generated by
`tools/sync-feature-alias.py`. `presentation.html` is the manually advanced
presentation and `presentation.pdf` its static draft. The previous companion
remains at `research.html`, and earlier visual studies at `samples/`. Font licences
are included in `fonts/`.

The website edition of 5 November 2026 follows Script v3 (checked 2 November 2026):
at least fourteen individual and group conversations, three Shenzhen trips and a
Sheung Shui walk, read alongside published evidence. Its miniature is authored
illustration, not measured geography; each building stands for a place that appears
in an account, an observation or a published figure. Public account descriptions
live in `feature/cast.js`. The optional route game uses invented demand and rules.
Field photographs were supplied by Bob and Jamie. Notes on this edition, checks and
porting: `docs/WEBSITE-EDITION-20261105.md`.

The original r2154 presentation PDF remains at presentation-r2154.pdf. Maps, policy
documents and research collections extend the main narrative with their own sources
and limits. This is a working draft for review, not a final approval by Jamie.
